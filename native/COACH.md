# AI coach and guided Watch workouts

## Eight-week shift calendar

Overview → Your eight weeks shows the upcoming week and opens the complete 56-day calendar. Create the first block with 17 October 2026 as both the block start and first morning shift, in Europe/Amsterdam. The repeating roster is two mornings, two afternoons, two nights and four days off. The default five training days are cycle days 2, 5, 8, 9 and 10: after the second morning, before the first night, and the final three days off. This first block runs through 11 December, with 27 training slots and a review on 12 December. The dates and cycle-day choices remain editable.

The calendar uses your saved program's sessions in continuous order; it does not invent a program or reset upper/lower rotation at the start of a week or shift cycle. Without a program it shows training slots and links to **Plan with coach**. That action opens a program discussion using the profile, calendar and training history; it does not request today's energy, sleep, soreness or other daily check-in answers. Future exercise targets are previews of the saved template. Fresh targets and readiness are checked when starting today's workout. Actual workout history controls rotation: missed dates do not create catch-up sessions, and future labels can shift after missed or extra sessions. The eight-week boundary is a review point, not an automatic program replacement.

Tap a day for its shift, projected session and base exercise targets, or recorded training. Upcoming training days can move to unoccupied recovery dates; individual dates can also switch between training and recovery. Past days and recorded workout days are protected. Calendar edits clear unstarted workout and program proposals while retaining the discussion. Review a new proposal before accepting it; daily check-in remains part of starting a workout. New block settings replace the calendar only; completed workout history remains unchanged.

The saved time zone and calendar-day arithmetic keep the roster aligned across daylight-saving changes. The calendar is included in native JSON backups and sent to both coach modes when asking for coaching. It takes precedence over an approximate days-per-week profile setting for its date range. The Watch receives bounded active-workout or ready-session snapshots, not the full calendar. For a saved program, the ready snapshot contains the next session's identity and exercise count; today's exercise targets are prepared after the Watch check-in.

## Personal profile, program and continuity

Settings → Profile saves your confirmed goals, experience, height, dated bodyweight measurements, training days, session time, equipment/increments, preferences and ongoing limitations. Daily readiness and workout feedback remain separate. You can edit or clear the profile; changing it invalidates draft acceptance and replies based on older state. Profile data and feedback are included in readable JSON backups. They are sent directly to OpenAI only when you request coaching; the Watch does not receive the profile or full training archive. `store: false` is a request setting, not a promise of zero provider retention.

Coach can propose a recurring program with rotating sessions, effort targets and an explicit progression rule. In program planning, accepting a program saves its version and makes those sessions available to the calendar. It does not prepare a daily workout or record readiness. When you choose **Start next workout**, the phone collects today's check-in once, prepares the next session offline, and shows the targets for review. **Accept & start** records that check-in and its timestamped energy/soreness together with the workout. No second readiness form follows the review. The saved program and its planning conversation stay unchanged.

From that preview, **Ask coach to adjust** optionally opens a daily workout discussion using the check-in already entered. Use it to discuss pain, soreness, time limits or other changes before starting. This replaces the current coach conversation with the daily discussion while leaving the saved recurring program unchanged. Accepting the coach's daily proposal returns to the workout preview with its adjusted targets and the original check-in/readiness. If the discussion lasts more than five minutes, explicitly confirm that the displayed check-in is still current before starting, or edit it if anything changed. Accepting a proposal never refreshes readiness automatically. **Review again** explicitly rebuilds from the saved program and replaces daily coach adjustments.

Known working sets, actual effort, comparable prescriptions and the saved increment govern load proposals. Unknown effort never counts as success. Long gaps require load reassessment; pain feedback, poor energy and high soreness inhibit progression. Short-time check-ins preserve the first-priority exercises and trim the day's draft using an approximate duration calculation, leaving the saved program unchanged. A missed day never adds catch-up sets. A finished session with no classified working sets does not advance rotation.

Overview and Coach link to Your program. Inspect all sessions, edit progression rules, discuss a complete revision, archive a program or restore a previous version. Each completed workout retains its program/session/version link. Changes do not rewrite actual training.

The coach receives a bounded context assembled from the full retained history: confirmed profile, saved program, locally computed next-target recommendations, rolling 7/28-day direct and secondary muscle sets, relevant exercise exposures (including those older than the six latest sessions), recent reviews and dated feedback. Raw old check-in notes are still excluded. The app does not train a personal model or promote transient soreness into permanent medical facts. Counts and progression are computed in Swift; the model explains, asks questions and proposes reviewed changes.

Finishing on either device saves a local review automatically. History shows planned versus actual work, missing effort and proposals for the next comparable session. Add reasons for skipped work or discomfort separately, and use Discuss workout for AI review. Completed-workout chat is read-only for training records. More recent pain feedback takes precedence over a finish-time progression proposal.

Settings → Exercise library supports custom lifts outside workouts and adds cable, Smith and machine variants. You can also describe an unfamiliar machine or movement in Coach without knowing its formal name. The coach asks for missing identifying details and can propose up to six library additions for review, with names, muscle estimates, equipment, weight convention and explanations. Tap **Save exercises & continue** to save those entries before the coach uses their app-assigned IDs in a program or workout. Correct suggestions in chat before saving. This is a separate action from accepting a program; merely receiving a suggestion never changes the library. Existing matching exercises are reused, and unknown equipment or weight conventions stay unspecified.

Metadata distinguishes direct/secondary muscles, equipment and load meaning. Use separate identities for different machines/setups. Once a custom lift has been used, changing its equipment or load convention requires a new variant; muscle-tag/name corrections apply to historical entries. Unclassified sets and warm-ups are excluded from muscle working-set counts. Indirect involvement is displayed separately, not treated as measured stimulus. Estimated 1RM is shown only for eligible classified sets with known high effort and a suitable load convention, never as a tested maximum.

Training principles and their limits are recorded in `GymaCore/CoachContext.swift`, with links to ACSM 2026, a repetition/load progression trial and an effort/failure trial. Numeric progression thresholds and the 28-day reassessment trigger are configurable/product heuristics, not claims of an individually optimal physiological prescription. Health history and wearable recovery scores are not required or collected.

## Use the app

1. In iPhone Settings → AI coach, save your personal OpenAI API key. Gyma uses `gpt-6.1-sol`; the API project must have access and API billing. Keys stay in this iPhone's Keychain, excluded from JSON backups and Watch sync.
2. Save your goals, equipment and preferences in Settings → Profile. Set up the calendar, then choose **Plan with coach**, or open Coach → **Plan program with coach**. Discuss the recurring sessions using your long-term goals, equipment, schedule and history. Daily energy, sleep and soreness are not requested or sent as a current check-in during program planning. Messages, profile, program, calendar, exercise catalog, relevant training summaries and feedback are sent directly to OpenAI when you request a reply. Past check-in notes are excluded. API requests use `store: false`.
3. Review the proposed program, including rotating sessions and progression rules, and accept it to save the program. Each exercise has working sets, reps, optional target kilograms and rest seconds. Gyma validates the program against the exercise library and saved profile before accepting it. Missing loads mean you choose the load; zero means bodyweight. Saving the program does not start a workout or create an accepted daily plan.
4. When ready to train, choose **Start next workout** from Overview or Your program. Check in with shift, energy, available time, optional sleep/notes and soreness for chest, back, legs, shoulders, arms and core. Review the resulting session, then tap **Accept & start**. The current energy/soreness selections are form defaults until you confirm them; earlier readiness is not reused. A preview expires after five minutes, at midnight, or when relevant app state changes, requiring a fresh review. On a calendar training day, Watch **Start Workout** offers the next saved-program session when today's slot has no recorded workout and no separate daily draft supersedes it. Confirm current energy and soreness there once; the phone uses the calendar shift and usual session duration to prepare that session. Watch start waits for a phone acknowledgment, expires after five minutes, cannot cross midnight, and rejects stale or duplicate starts. The Coach cannot start or finish a workout through chat.
5. On Watch, the first exercise opens with editable **KG** and expected **REPS**. Choose them, tap **Start Set**, perform it, then tap **Finish Set**. Adjust completed reps with the **− / reps / +** buttons and tap **Confirm Reps**. On the next screen, scroll the kilogram wheel with the Digital Crown in **0.5 kg steps**, then tap **Confirm Weight**. The back arrow returns to reps without losing the weight. For working sets, Confirm Weight opens Effort: Several more reps, 1–2 more reps, At my limit, or Not sure. The set is saved after this choice. Select Warm-up before starting to exclude it from working targets; warm-ups save after weight confirmation and skip effort. The same rep buttons and kilogram wheel are used when preparing a set or changing the next set during rest. Existing weights between the half-kilogram steps stay selectable. Rest starts after confirmation, except after the final target set of an exercise. Tap **Dismiss** when you resume; it records the elapsed rest using the Watch tap time and begins the next set after confirmation from iPhone. Change the next set's weight or reps during rest if needed. The next exercise opens with its own preparation controls.
6. Allow Gyma's workout access when opening an active session on Watch. Gyma uses a native strength-training `HKWorkoutSession` to keep the session running with the wrist down and expose the system workout icon for returning to the app. The display can still dim or follow watchOS/user settings. Gyma does not read your Health history or save a duplicate Health workout; the system may generate health samples during the session.
7. **Rest vibration is on by default**, independent of notification permission. During the native workout session, six full notification-haptic pulses spaced two seconds apart mark the rest deadline, including in the background. Later pulses stop when rest is dismissed, changed, or ended; the sequence lasts about ten seconds. WatchKit controls individual pulse intensity and duration. The app requests no notification sound; turn on Watch Silent Mode to mute any system tones attached to built-in haptics. The exact Mindfulness/Breathe pattern is not exposed to apps. If workout mode is unavailable, Gyma uses foreground haptics plus an optional silent visual notification fallback. Rest remains visible until **Dismiss**. Watch settings includes **Test vibration**, the vibration toggle and Finish workout early. A settings badge flags workout-mode permission or alert issues; open settings to read the details.

Inside the iPhone workout, **Ask coach** opens a separate conversation using live exercises, targets, actual sets, rest and readiness. Ask for advice, different target weights/reps/rest, or a replacement for an exercise that has no logged sets. Proposed changes have an explicit **Apply change** action; stale responses and proposals are rejected if the workout changed. Completed sets remain intact. The coach cannot start or finish workouts through chat.

History records timestamped readiness alongside the workout's check-in. Exercise Progress shows energy and that exercise's muscle soreness beside each session. Older workouts without readiness stay unrecorded, rather than being treated as no soreness. Recent sessions and relevant older exercise exposures, together with full-history analytics, supply context; this is not automatic model training or proof that a condition caused progress.

Existing backups, workout-planning conversations and accepted single-workout plans remain compatible. Conversations without an explicit purpose keep their original workout-planning behavior, including the earlier check-in and separate pre-start readiness confirmation. Earlier plans without a scheduled date use their creation day. Existing accepted plans remain startable on their scheduled day; revising them requires accepting again. Starting a fresh saved-program session on iPhone supersedes an unstarted legacy daily draft. New program discussions omit daily check-in data from AI context even though a compatibility placeholder remains in the stored conversation format. Older Watch commands keep their original encoding and duplicate-detection identity.

The phone remains the authoritative store. Watch changes are saved to a durable outbox and wait for a phone receipt before another mutation. Keep the paired iPhone available. A disconnected Watch is not an independent multi-set workout recorder.

## Validation

Continuity tests cover legacy backup decoding, profile constraints/privacy, program acceptance/linking/version changes, warm-up/effort persistence, delayed acknowledgements, classified volume and missing metadata, comparable progression, nil-load calibration, long gaps, empty-session rotation, completed-workout advice, stale profile/program replies, and conservative progression after pain feedback. Program-planning tests cover missing daily readiness in requests, rejecting daily-plan replies, saving a program without a daily draft, and preserving legacy conversation behavior. Daily-start tests cover fresh phone check-ins, unchanged program discussions, calendar-aware Watch availability, stale state/readiness rejection, persistence, command replay, midnight expiry and legacy ready snapshots. New views are included through the existing XcodeGen source directories.

Additional device checks: save/reopen/clear Profile; record dated bodyweights; accept a program; prepare and shorten a session offline; record warm-ups then working sets with each effort choice and Not sure; relaunch during effort review; finish on Watch and inspect the phone review; add discomfort feedback; discuss a completed workout; edit/restore program rules; inspect direct/secondary volume; restore an older backup. Live API schema and device layout require actual API/device testing after the macOS build.

Shared tests cover legacy data decoding, acceptance and revisions, invalid AI plans, structured response parsing, original/actual rest durations, delayed and duplicate commands, progress in bounded snapshots, persisted Watch set phases, and rest-haptic scheduling. The existing `swift.yml` workflow tests GymaCore and builds both native apps.

Device checks:

- Add/replace/remove a key. Create a calendar and choose Plan with coach; verify no daily check-in form appears. Request program changes, accept, and relaunch. Confirm the calendar has recurring sessions but no daily workout or readiness record was created. Start from iPhone, enter today's check-in, inspect targets and Accept & start; verify only one check-in was requested and the saved program discussion remains available.
- From the daily preview, choose Ask coach to adjust, discuss a limitation, and accept the daily proposal. Confirm the preview keeps the same check-in and the accepted targets. Let more than five minutes elapse: targets must remain visible, starting must stay disabled until explicit readiness confirmation, and Review again must explain that it rebuilds the session. Close or cancel the discussion and verify stale previews cannot start; a program proposal must never be accepted as a daily workout.
- With a saved program, verify Watch offers the next session on an unused training day and asks for current energy/soreness once before starting. Check rest days, existing daily drafts, completed calendar slots, midnight, stale/replaced programs, delayed delivery and duplicate start commands. Restore an older accepted single-workout plan and verify its original scheduled start path still works.
- Test invalid key, unavailable network and retry; the current draft and pending message must survive without duplicate chat messages. Restoring a backup or replacing a check-in during a request must discard a stale response.
- Select kilograms before starting, tap Finish Set, use − for one fewer actual rep and Confirm Reps, scroll the kilogram wheel to a half-kilogram value, then Confirm Weight. Check Back preserves values and the weight wheel retains an existing quarter-kilogram value until changed. Verify actual performance saves once, rest appears, Dismiss begins the next set, and the next exercise appears after the target number of working sets. Relaunch while preparing, lifting, reviewing reps, reviewing weight and awaiting confirmation; values and phase must remain intact.
- Acknowledge rest early and late. History should show actual versus original planned seconds. Extend rest on iPhone; the original plan remains recorded.
- Inside an active workout, request tips, a target adjustment and an unstarted exercise replacement. Apply each proposal and check Watch updates; changes to completed sets must be rejected. Log a Watch set while waiting for an AI reply and verify stale advice/proposals cannot overwrite it.
- Test vibration with Watch foreground, wrist down/background, Focus enabled, and notification permission denied. Allow Health workout access, check the workout icon, and verify Test vibration. Deny Health access separately and check the fallback/error state. Check no duplicate deadline taps on reopening or refreshing, and verify workout mode stops on phone finish or a queued Watch finish.
- Reconnect after a queued set or rest acknowledgment. Review rejection messages if the phone session changed meanwhile. Reopen older data and restore a previous-format JSON backup.

Live AI calls need a user-supplied API key. Physical pairing, haptic delivery and on-device layout require an iPhone/Watch check after signing and installing the combined IPA locally. Workout mode requires the signed Watch provisioning profile to grant `com.apple.developer.healthkit`. The custom iLoader now enables that capability when the Watch bundle requests workout runtime and checks the profile before signing. This capability update needs the updated local installer; subsequent ordinary app changes do not require rebuilding it.

API references: [GPT-6.1 Sol](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6.1-sol), [Structured Outputs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs?api-mode=responses).
